# Fiona

Fiona was a direct to consumer web application which allowed users to search and compare personalized financial products, access their search history, and explore educational content about products and our network. Fiona allowed Even Financial to dogfood new products and API features before launching them to the public, as well as to leverage SEO and SEM channels to acquire new customers.


## Role

I was the Director of Consumer Products, directly responsible for both the engineering and product development of Fiona. I oversaw a team of 7 developers and designers who worked with me to develop and maintain the application. 


### Goal

Fiona was designed to be a performant, maintainable application which could be easily adapted to expand to new markets and products, while allowing for end user accounts and compliant product experiences. 

### Architecture

1. An SSR Frontend Application
2. A CMS usable by Marketing to manage all content and text on the site
3. An Identity Service to manage user accounts and authentication
4. A set of TypeScript microservices to manage backend workflows
   1. Search Service
   2. Messaging Service
   3. User Service

### Technologies

1. Next.js / React
2. Directus
3. Keycloak
4. Loopback / Typescript
5. Postgres
