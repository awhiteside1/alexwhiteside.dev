# Credit Card Vertical

The Credit Card Vertical enabled us to search, compare and recommend credit cards from our partners to our customers. It included administration, inventory management, frontend experiences, and conversion reporting, and leveraged the Rewards Model and Simulation Engine ported from Birch Finance which was acquired by Even Financial. 

## Role

As the CTO of Birch Finance I adapted our model and engine to expose an API suitable for integration, as well as advised stakeholders on card recommendation and conversion information from our user data.
