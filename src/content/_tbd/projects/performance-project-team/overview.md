# Performance Project Team

A project team focused on improving overall performance for end users across our web applications. It focused on 
educating developers and product managers, monitoring and auditing our production environments, and documenting 
proven approaches within our systems. 

## Role

I founded and led this project team until it was merged into the Core Network Frontend initiative.
